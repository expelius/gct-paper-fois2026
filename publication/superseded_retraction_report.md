## 5. Honest framing

This is **defeasibility-lite**, not full defeasible reasoning. The maxim engine still asserts conclusions monotonically. What the snapshot now carries is *retraction metadata* — explicit supersession edges plus a downstream-consumer index — that any external reasoner (a default-logic layer, an argumentation framework, a manual reviewer) can use as an attack relation. Full defeasibility would require integrating that attack relation into the maxim engine itself, so that `missing_specification` proposals on consumers of a superseded experiment are emitted with `confidence: defeated_by(X)` rather than as ordinary positive proposals. That extension is straightforward but beyond the scope of the present submission.
